# Sandbox Tower

## Short description

*(146 characters)*

> Build a sandcastle, arm it with toy blasters, and hold the gate. Every wave of garden bugs hits harder — rebuild between them or watch it crumble.

## Description

*(518 characters)*

Build a sandcastle out of the blocks you are dealt, then defend it.
Between waves you get a hand of four shapes — sand walls, dart blasters, squirt guns, mud mortars, bug zappers — and a short window to place them. Then the swarm arrives: ants, cockroaches, spitting spiders, roly-polies and toy-junk contraptions that out-range everything you own.
Blocks that lose their support fall, and falling sand crushes whatever is underneath. Spend sand, pebbles and gold, unlock the tech tree, and see how tall you can build.

## How to play

**Build, then hold.** Each wave has two halves: a short build phase, then the fight. You cannot build while the swarm is on the field, so what you place is what you get.

1. **Take a shape from the tray.** You are dealt four at a time. Slot one is always a structure piece, slot two is always a weapon, the other two are free. A shape you do not want can be rerolled.
2. **Place it on the sandcastle.** Blocks need support — anything that ends up disconnected from the Castle Gate falls, takes damage on landing, and crushes bugs it lands on. The ground row is limited to four cells either side of the Gate; higher floors can spread as wide as you like.
3. **Watch what the wave brings.** Each bug punishes a different lazy sandcastle. Cockroaches close before long-range blasters can whittle them; spiders stand off and out-range melee; flyers ignore the ground entirely; bombardier beetles open a hole in a wall of cheap sand; toy-junk contraptions park beyond every blaster you own.
4. **Call the wave early for a bonus.** Unused build time converts to extra resources — up to +40%. Calling early is a real trade, not a skip button.
5. **Protect the Gate.** Losing blocks is survivable and expected. Losing the Castle Gate ends the run.

### Resources

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sand** | The cheap, fast material. Walls, packed sand, dart blasters. |
| **Pebbles** | Scarcer. Heavier structure and the bigger guns. |
| **Gold** | Dropped by kills. Pays for the advanced blocks — mud mortar, balloon launcher, bug zapper, ice pop, bucket & spade, mint — on top of their sand and pebble cost. |
| **Coins** | Banked between runs. Spent in the tech tree on permanent upgrades. |

### Tips

- **Weapons make bad walls.** A sandcastle of nothing but toy blasters folds the moment anything reaches it.
- **Height is not safety.** Bumblebees cruise above the castle and drop honey bombs straight down.
- **A clean wave makes the next one harder.** Two waves without losing a single block raises the difficulty; taking damage resets it.
- **Toy Knights are the answer to standoff contraptions.** Nothing on the sandcastle reaches a pencil trebuchet.
- **The tech tree persists.** A run that ends badly still pays into the next one.

## Controls

Everything is playable with one hand on a phone, or with a mouse on desktop.

| Action | Touch | Mouse / keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Pick a shape | Tap a tray slot | Click a tray slot |
| Place it | Tap a highlighted cell | Click a highlighted cell |
| Preview a shape's stats | Long-press a tray slot | Hover a tray slot |
| Reroll a shape | Tap the reroll arrow on a slot | Click the reroll arrow |
| Inspect or sell a block | Tap a placed block | Click a placed block |
| Pan the battlefield | Drag | Drag |
| Zoom | Pinch | Scroll wheel |
| Recentre the camera | Tap the recentre button | Click the recentre button |
| Start the wave | Tap **Call Wave** | Click **Call Wave** |
| Double speed | Tap the **2×** button | Click the **2×** button |
| Summon Toy Knights | Tap the Toy Knights button | Click the Toy Knights button |

The camera recentres itself unless you have panned away — the recentre button only appears once you have.
