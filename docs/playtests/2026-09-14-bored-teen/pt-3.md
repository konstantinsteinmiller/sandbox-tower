# Playtester 3 — desktop, persona: 13yo portal browser, five tabs open, zero patience

## The first twenty seconds
Load-in was a still shot: mountains, a little wall-and-gate castle, "MONSTERS INCOMING!" text sitting over it, wave counter at 0, and a resource bar (wood 120 / stone 40 / gold 0). Nothing was moving except a glowing yellow down-arrow pointing at a sword icon in the bottom tray. I clicked it because it was the only thing telling me to do anything. That opened a merge-chart popup (cannon + cannon = bigger cannon, four tiers of it) that I had to click through, then a side-by-side stat comparison card (-10% vs -75% damage) that also just sat there. By 20.3s I still hadn't placed anything that shot at anything or seen a single monster. It was all static tutorial cards with a "CLICK TO CONTINUE" prompt. Nothing in those 20 seconds was a reason to stay — no motion, no enemy, no noise cue, just menus.

## Did I quit? When and why
Around 20s I logged my quit point in my head: this is a builder-tutorial with training wheels, not a game, and if the actual portal thumbnail had promised action I'd have already bounced back to the grid. In real life I give a game 20 seconds and this one spent all of them on popups. I kept playing past that only because I was told to.

## Second by second — what I saw and what I did
- 0–3s: static "MONSTERS INCOMING" screen, arrow pointing at tray item.
- 3–20s: clicked the highlighted item twice, placed two cannon pieces on the wall, got shown the SAME merge-chart popup and the SAME -10%/-75% comparison card twice in a row. Zero monsters on screen.
- 20–33s: more forced tutorial — click cannon, see stats, click gate, click "CALL WAVE" because an arrow told me to.
- 33–40s: wave 1 finally starts (6 enemies). Cannon fires, explosion with damage numbers (+13, +2, +2). This is the first real "something is happening" moment, at 33s in.
- 40–44s: "WAVE 1 HELD!" — a flying winged chest floats up as a reward. Fun little beat.
- 44–65s: back to build screen. Tried to place a free mortar reward — it visually got "stuck" following my cursor as a red ghost outline through several clicks and screenshots before it actually locked in. Confusing.
- 65–99s: waves 2 and 3 auto-resolve while I build. Full health held both times. Day/night cycle visibly shifts (daylight → sunset → dusk) in the background, which is a nice touch, but it's decorative — nothing about it affects what I'm doing.
- 99–132s: a badge popped on a UI icon — opened it, it was a full "TECH TREE" permanent-upgrade screen. Meta-progression I didn't ask for and don't care about at 13, closed it immediately.
- 132–144s: dead stretch — sitting on the build screen with the same two red-ghost cannon squares stuck to my cursor, countdown barely ticking. Nothing to do.
- 144–178s: manually forced "CALL WAVE" to skip the dead air. Waves 4 and 5 ran (19 enemies each), still never took a scratch of damage, moon rose, more reward chests. Session ended at 178s with the castle still at full HP the entire run.

## When was I bored, exactly
- 0–33s: worst stretch. Nothing but tutorial popups repeating themselves (the merge chart and the damage-comparison card each showed twice, identical). A skip button or "got it, don't show again" would fix this instantly.
- 132–144s: a second dead patch where the build screen just sat there with a stuck placement ghost and no enemies, no countdown urgency, nothing to click that changed anything. Fixed by not letting the wave-call timer idle silently — flash it harder or auto-start sooner.
- General: the castle never even got scratched across 5 waves. Zero danger the entire session makes the "combat" parts boring too, not just the menu parts.

## What did I not understand
- Whether the wood/stone/gold I was collecting from kills actually mattered or I was just hoarding it — never got explained mid-fight.
- The mortar/cannon placement kept showing a red-outlined ghost icon stuck to my mouse after I'd apparently already placed one. I couldn't tell if I'd placed one extra copy, none, or if it was just visual noise. Didn't care enough to dig into why — I just clicked past it.
- The gate's "armour 4" stat and the separate currency icon (a little crown/star coin, different from wood/stone/gold) never got explained. Didn't try to find out.

## What was actually cool
The flying winged treasure chest after wave 1 was a genuine "oh, nice" — it floats up out of the fight with a little flourish and pops coins. The day-night sky (sunrise to sunset to full moon) over the course of a run is legitimately pretty for a browser game and I did notice it. Everything else was fine but not exciting.

## Would I click this thumbnail again
No. The core loop (watch a cannon auto-fire, wait for a countdown, occasionally drag a piece onto a wall) never once threatened me — never lost health in 5 waves — so there's no tension driving me to keep tapping. And the first 30 seconds are pure menu/tutorial with no monster on screen, which is exactly the kind of thumbnail I'd back out of before the video finishes buffering on my other tab.
