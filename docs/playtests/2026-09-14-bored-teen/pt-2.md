# Playtester 2 — desktop, persona: 14yo tower-defence player, hunting for a real decision

## Did I quit? When and why
I hit my real quit point around **121 s**, right after Wave 5 held. Five waves, something like 65+ enemies total, and my base health bar had not dropped a single pixel. In real life I'd have closed the tab there — not because nothing was happening (stuff was always exploding on screen), but because nothing I did seemed to be under any pressure. I kept playing per instructions through Wave 9 (18 enemies, ~178.8 s), and the pattern never broke: the base never took damage once, the whole run.

## Second by second — what I saw and what I did
- **0–10 s**: Game opens on "MONSTERS INCOMING!" with a tutorial arrow. I place a basic cannon on the wall, then a popup shows me the merge tree (2 cannons → lvl2 → lvl3 → lvl4 → lvl5) and a demo comparing a lvl1 cannon's damage (-10% enemy HP) to a lvl2 cannon's (-75%). Clear, good teaching moment.
- **10–20 s**: Place a second cannon, it auto-merges into a lvl2 "Cannon (Merged 2x)" — 120 HP, 48 dmg. I hit UPGRADE (24 materials) → LV1/5, 156 HP, 59 dmg. Upgraded the Gate too (8 materials → 390 HP, armour 4).
- **23 s**: I call Wave 1 (7 enemies) manually — arrow was pointing at the button anyway.
- **23–45 s**: Wave 1 clears itself while I watch. A flying chest appears top-right; I click it and get a free card. I place Archery, a Stone Block, a Wood Crate, a Banner (buffs neighbours), and a free Lightning Coil (chains between enemies — looked cool).
- **50 s**: Poked the Tech Tree (green diamond icon, arrow pointed at it) — a big permanent meta-upgrade tree, locked behind a currency I only had 10 of.
- **65 s**: Checked Daily Missions (clipboard icon, had a badge) — claimed 40 coins for "survive to Wave 2."
- **70–120 s**: Waves 2, 3, 4 called and cleared (8, 18, 25 enemies) at 3x speed. Base health bar: still full every time. Resources climbing fast (wood/stone/materials all into the 100s).
- **121 s — QUIT POINT.**
- **121–161 s**: Waves 5, 6, 7, 8 cleared, still zero damage. Tried to merge two same-tier cannons by dragging one onto the other — it just panned the camera, no merge happened; I had to place new pieces directly on/next to a tower at placement time instead. Bought the "Foundations" tech-tree node (+50% starting tower HP, permanent) once I had 199 coins.
- **161–178.8 s**: Wave 9 (18 enemies) starts, wolves and a flying bat-type enemy show up with visible HP numbers over their heads — first time I saw enemy HP displayed like that. Session ends here, base still at full health.

## When was I bored, exactly
- **121 s onward (the big one):** not "nothing happening on screen" — cannons were firing, chests were popping, numbers were flying — but **nothing was mine to decide**. Once towers are placed, combat runs itself; there's no target-priority, no ability to trigger, nothing to dodge or time. It's a slot-filling loop, not a battle I'm fighting.
- **50–65 s:** the Tech Tree and Daily Missions menus were neat to find but I couldn't act on either (not enough currency, mission already tracked itself) — they felt like previews of a game I'd play tomorrow, not today.

## Did my choices matter
Concretely: I upgraded my first cannon from 120→156 HP and 48→59 damage, and later bought "Foundations" (+50% starting tower HP, permanent across runs) for 20 coins. Both changes showed up as real numbers on the panel. But I could never tell if they **mattered** — the base took zero damage whether my towers were upgraded or not, across 9 straight waves. I never saw a wave get close to breaching, never saw a "close call," never had to choose between two towers because I couldn't afford both — resources outpaced my spending the whole time (ended around 265 wood / 208 stone / 261 materials, mostly unspent). The missing feedback is a **failure state or a near-miss** — some sign that a weaker build would have actually lost. Without that, upgrading just makes numbers go up for their own sake.

## What did I not understand
- Whether cannons can be merged **after** placement — I tried dragging one onto another to combine them and it just panned the camera. Never found the right gesture, and honestly didn't care enough to keep hunting for it once combat had proven itself trivial.
- What "Rank" on the Tech Tree nodes actually does long-term, or what the other two Tech Tree branches (locked, greyed out) unlock — didn't explore because I only had spare currency once, near the very end.
- Why some enemies show a floating HP number (the wolves, wave 9) and others don't — looked like an elite/tougher-enemy tag but nothing explained it on screen.

## What was actually cool
The flying chest bonus (click it before it floats off for a free card) was a nice little "stay alert" moment — genuinely the only time the game asked me to do something with a clock on it besides the wave timer. The Lightning Coil's chain-lightning visual arcing between two goblins looked great. And the merge-tree tutorial popup at the very start (showing -10% vs -75% damage side by side) was a genuinely good way to teach the core hook in five seconds.

## Would I play again tomorrow
No. If the first thing that happens in nine straight waves is "my castle is never in danger," there's no reason to come back and try to do better — there's nothing to do better *at*. Give me one wave where I'm one bad placement away from losing the gate and I'm hooked; right now it just plays itself while I watch.
