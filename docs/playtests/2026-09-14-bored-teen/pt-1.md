# Playtester 1 — desktop, persona: 11yo Roblox/Brawl Stars kid, low patience

## Did I quit? When and why

I never actually hit a real quit point. I got kind of bored a couple times (the tutorial arrows dragging on around 5-30s, and the empty countdown gaps between waves like 92-98s and 130-140s) but the game kept throwing a new wave or a new shiny thing at me before I gave up, so I never closed the tab for real. Closest I got was during the tech tree screen around 62-64s — too many tiny icons and I only had 10 coins so I couldn't buy anything, that felt like a dead end, but I clicked out of it and the game just kept going.

## Second by second — what I saw and what I did

- **0-2s**: Screen says "MONSTERS INCOMING!" with a big yellow arrow pointing at a card. Clicked it.
- **2-6s**: Tower lights up a spot, I click it, places a cannon.
- **6-24s**: A "merge chart" pops up twice showing cannon+cannon=bigger cannon, then a little fight demo with two goblins and health bars comparing a weak cannon (-10%) to a strong one (-75%). Kind of neat but it's basically a slideshow, I just mashed "click to continue."
- **13-20s**: Opened the cannon's stat card (HP/DAMAGE/COOLDOWN/RANGE/SPLASH) and hit UPGRADE. Also accidentally opened the GATE stats and upgraded that too.
- **36s**: Hit "CALL WAVE" — wave 1 starts, 6 enemies, cannon auto-fires, damage numbers pop up. This is the first bit that felt like a real game.
- **39-45s**: Turned on 3x speed. Wave clears fast, a flying chest with wings shows up out of nowhere, that was cool.
- **47-53s**: Placed an archer tower, then went through the SAME merge-chart tutorial screen a second time. Kind of repetitive.
- **62-64s**: Opened the Tech Tree by accident (green diamond button) — big grid of tiny icons, only had 10 coins, couldn't afford the one thing it showed me (20 coins for +50% HP). Closed it.
- **77-81s**: Found "Daily Missions" (clipboard icon had a red badge) — claimed 40 coins for "survive to wave 2." Free stuff, liked that.
- **83-85s**: Went back to Tech Tree, now had 62 coins, bought the Foundations upgrade. Little sparkle, more icons unlocked.
- **92-98s**: Just sitting there watching a "CALL WAVE 0:xx" countdown tick down with nothing to click. Kind of dead air.
- **98-107s**: Wave 3, 18 enemies, set to 3x speed, watched explosions and coin numbers fly. Wave cleared, got a "FREE" cannon card.
- **109-115s**: Tried to place the free cannon like 3 times, clicked all around the tower, no red squares ever showed up to let me place it. Never figured out why. Gave up on it.
- **119-126s**: Wave 4, 20 enemies, moon rises (day/night cycle), lots of explosions both sides of the tower, "WAVE 4 HELD!" banner.
- **140-150s**: Wave 5, 20 enemies, more explosions, coins climbing fast (102→108 wood, etc).
- **163-176s**: Wave 6, 19 enemies, a new bigger enemy shows up on the left (looked like a wolf/bear thing, not just goblins), cleared it too.
- **176-179s**: Time's up. Still mid-game, wave 6 just held.

## When was I bored, exactly

- **5-24s (tutorial merge-chart popups)**: My hands weren't doing anything except mashing "click to continue" on two slideshow screens back to back. It's not a game yet, it's a slide deck. Fix: let me just play and learn by doing, or make the demo skippable after the first time.
- **62-64s (Tech Tree)**: Opened a menu with like 40 tiny icons and I could afford literally one node. Nothing to actually pick between. Fix: don't show me a huge locked tree when I have 10 coins, show me the one thing I can afford, big and obvious.
- **92-98s and 130-140s (wave countdown gaps)**: Just staring at a number ticking down with nothing to click. My hands are just sitting on the mouse doing nothing. Fix: let me place blocks or spend coins during the countdown instead of after, or shorten the gap.

## What did I not understand

- The "FREE" cannon card — I tried clicking it and then clicking all over the tower and no spot ever lit up red to let me place it. I don't know if it was already used, or if there was no valid spot, or if I was clicking wrong. I cared enough to try like 3 times but then gave up and just let the wave start.
- The tech tree — too many icons, no idea what most of them do, didn't hover to check because that's a lot of reading.
- Why the gate panel kept popping up when I was trying to click the cannon — I clicked once and got the gate stats instead, confusing hitboxes.

## What was actually cool

- The explosions when cannons hit enemies, with the little floating damage numbers and gold numbers popping off — that's satisfying, especially at 3x speed with a big wave.
- The flying chest with wings that showed up randomly after wave 1 — shiny, unexpected, I liked that.
- The day/night/moon cycle in the background — didn't expect that, looked nice.
- Wave 6 had a different, bigger enemy (not just goblins) — glad they're not all the same.
- Getting free coins from the daily mission just for surviving to wave 2.

## Would I play again tomorrow

Yeah, probably, but I'd want it to skip straight to a wave instead of making me sit through the merge-chart tutorial and menus again. The actual wave fights with cannons blowing stuff up are fun, especially with speed cranked up. The in-between menu stuff (tech tree, waiting for the countdown) is where I'd start tabbing away to something else if it went on much longer.
