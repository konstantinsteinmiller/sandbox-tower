# Playtester 6 — mobile, persona: hyper-casual phone player

## Summary (5 lines max)
A tower-defense/merge game where you drag crate cards onto a vertical lane to build a tower that auto-fights waves of monsters. I never got a "how to play" screen — I figured out drag-to-place, merging, and waves entirely by watching a single glowing card and trial-and-error. The core loop (place block, watch it auto-fight, collect gold, repeat) is instantly graspable and satisfying, and daily missions plus a persistent star-coin upgrade shop gave real between-run motivation. But I never once saw or understood WHY I died — both runs ended with a sudden "Destroyed!" banner with no visible cause, no HP bar for my own structure, and several UI elements (a mystery "FREE" badge card, a 3x multiplier button, gate red-outline warnings) stayed unexplained the whole session.

## Run 1 — what happened (timeline with game-time stamps)
- **~0-4s (first impression):** Game opened directly into "Wave 1.1 — MONSTERS INCOMING!" with no title/menu/tutorial screen. Only cue was one glowing card at the bottom (cost 10).
- **~4-7s:** Tapped the glowing card, dragged it onto a highlighted lane — it placed a wooden crate and, within ~3 seconds, "WAVE 1 HELD!" appeared. I never saw a monster reach the crate or take damage.
- **~12s:** An unprompted "Merge Guide" overlay explained the merge chain (2 tier-1 crates → tier-2 green cannon → tier-3 blue → tier-4 purple → tier-5 orange via 3 dots). This was the single clearest piece of information in the whole session.
- **~13-30s:** Zoomed-out camera view highlighted a "VOLLEY" ability button with an arrow; tapping it fired a visible explosive attack at a flying enemy. Discovered the diamond icon opens a "BLUEPRINTS" permanent-upgrade shop (star-coin currency), bought Foundations (+50% tower HP).
- **~1 min:** Comfortably clearing waves 2-6, placing crates/blocks each wave, occasionally collecting flying "chest" rewards that granted free cards (some inert, some placeable).
- **~2:30 (146s):** After wave 7, the "CALL WAVE" button turned red and read "CALL BOSS" — first indication a boss existed. Called it; a tree-monster boss appeared and was defeated after visible chip damage.
- **~2:55 (175s):** A clipboard "Daily Missions" icon appeared for the first time (post-boss), with 3 already-complete missions worth 200 combined star coins — big motivating coin-shower moment.
- **~3-5 min:** Continued clearing waves 8-12, spending stars on Blueprint upgrades between waves (Foundations, Sharpened Bolts, Braced Crates) with clear diminishing-return cost scaling.
- **~4:56-5:15 (296-310s):** Around wave 13, screen edges showed a subtle red vignette and the gate got a red outline — the only "you're in danger" signal all game, with no numeric health shown. Reinforced with a crate, danger cleared temporarily.
- **~5:34 (334.8s): RUN 1 END.** Sudden "Destroyed!" banner — no visible breach, explosion, or specific cause shown. Result: "You survived to wave 13 — NEW RECORD, Rank #293 of 361, +264 star coins." A "WAVE 12" button (marked 1x) instantly revived the run with full resources restored — I used it, played ~15 more seconds, and died again at wave 13 with the same result screen (no continue offered the second time).

## Run 2 — what happened
Restarted from the Destroyed screen's blue restart icon. This time I spent accumulated star coins (538, carried over as permanent progression) immediately on Blueprint upgrades (Foundations, Sharpened Bolts, Bombard, Rapid Fire) before placing a single block, since Run 1 taught me stars persist between runs and upgrades compound. Played faster and more efficiently, using 1.5x game speed throughout, collecting all flying chests, buying Reinforced Hand refreshes, and calling the boss proactively at wave 7-8.
- Reached the wave-8 boss fight around 483-523s game time (about 40s of session time, much faster than Run 1's equivalent point, confirming pre-run upgrades matter).
- **~523s: RUN 2 END.** Destroyed again, this time only at wave 8 — no "NEW RECORD" banner, worse than Run 1 despite stronger starting upgrades, purely due to less careful/attentive play (I was watching more passively). Same "no visible cause of death" problem. Result screen added a new "3x star coin multiplier" purchase (30 coins) whose effect I could not visually confirm — the reward number never appeared to change after buying it.
- Did not use the continue-offer this time, to end the run cleanly.

What I didn't understand: what the ultimate goal is (survive forever? reach a specific wave? beat a final boss?), what the gate/door structure represents, or what "losing" even means structurally. When: 0s. How long unclear: never fully resolved — I inferred the goal (survive waves) only through play, never confirmed. Screenshot: 0002-wait.jpg. Severity: **high**.
2. **No visible health bar for my own structure/gate.** What I saw: towers, blocks, and enemies all had individual HP bars, but I never saw a number or bar for the overall base/gate health. What I didn't understand: how close I was to defeat at any moment. When: entire session. How long unclear: never resolved. Screenshot: 0096-wait.jpg (red vignette warning with no number). Severity: **high**.
3. **Death cause never shown.** What I saw: a sudden "Destroyed!" banner both times, with no slow-motion, no "X enemy broke through," no summary of what failed. What I didn't understand: which enemy, which lane, or which mechanic caused defeat, so I couldn't adjust strategy. When: ~335s (run 1) and ~523s (run 2). How long unclear: permanently — game never explains it. Screenshots: 0102-wait.jpg, 0135-wait.jpg. Severity: **high**.
4. **The "FREE" badge card that doesn't respond to taps.** What I saw: a green/gold card with a numbered badge sitting persistently next to the "Reinforced Hand" purchase button. What I didn't understand: tapping and dragging it repeatedly did nothing at first (it turned out other, similarly-styled "FREE" cards from chests WERE draggable — this one specifically never was). Confusing because visually identical UI pattern behaved two different ways. When: first appeared ~22s, unclear for the entire session. Screenshot: 0028-tap.jpg. Severity: **medium**.
5. **"CALL WAVE" bonus percentage and its logic.** What I saw: a countdown timer with a decaying "+X%" bonus next to the CALL WAVE button. What I didn't understand: what the bonus applies to (gold? stars? both?), and whether tapping early or waiting for 0 is better — I inferred by experiment that tapping forces the next wave, but the percentage's actual payoff was never shown as a before/after number. When: ~7s onward. How long unclear: entire session. Screenshot: 0017-wait.jpg. Severity: **medium**.
6. **Flying "chest" reward's cost was ambiguous.** What I saw: the very first chest I tapped cost 49 wood to open rather than being free. What I didn't understand: whether this was a purchase, a gamble, or a mistake on my part — no confirmation dialog or price shown before tapping. When: ~22s. How long unclear: resolved by the second chest (later ones seemed free), but the first interaction was confusing. Screenshot: 0014-tap.jpg. Severity: **low**.
7. **Post-death "3x" multiplier button's effect was invisible.** What I saw: tapping a "3x [star] — 30 [coin]" button on the Destroyed screen played a coin-shower animation, but the on-screen "+175" reward text never visibly updated to a tripled value, and I have no way to confirm what I actually got. When: ~524s. How long unclear: never resolved. Screenshot: 0136-tap.jpg, 0137-wait.jpg. Severity: **medium**.
8. **Resource icon changed appearance mid-game without explanation.** What I saw: the top-left wood/resource icon briefly rendered as a cloud/puff shape instead of the wood-crate icon (same number, different icon) around wave 5. What I didn't understand: whether this signaled a different resource or was a rendering glitch. When: ~85s. How long unclear: reverted on its own; never explained. Screenshot: 0045-wait.jpg. Severity: **low**.

## Incentive gaps (numbered)
1. **Between-run motivation is strong where it exists, but arrives too late.** The Daily Missions (with a satisfying coin-shower claim) and the persistent Blueprints star-shop are genuinely motivating — but Daily Missions only became visible after clearing the wave-8 boss in Run 1 (~175s in), meaning a player who dies before wave 8 (very plausible for a first-timer) never sees this hook at all. Severity: **high**.
2. **No forward-looking promise of what's next.** The game never previews what higher waves, unlocks, or the endgame look like (no "wave 20 unlocks X" teaser, no boss gallery, no visible tech tree beyond what's already unlocked in Blueprints). A player has no reason to imagine "just one more wave" beyond raw curiosity. Severity: **medium**.
3. **Rank number felt disconnected from effort.** "Rank #293 of 361" appeared identical after both runs (wave 13 and wave 8 respectively) even though performance differed — this undercut the feeling that rank reflects the specific run, making the leaderboard feel static/decorative rather than a live incentive. Severity: **medium**.
4. **The free "continue" (revive at last wave) is powerful but unexplained and used up before you understand it.** In Run 1 I only discovered it by curiosity-tapping a button that had no label indicating "revive." A first-time player under the shock of a sudden unexplained death is unlikely to experiment with an unlabeled button before deciding to quit. Severity: **high**.

## What worked (things that were clear or motivating)
- The Merge Guide overlay (2→1 crate merging chain) was immediately clear and visually well-designed — best single piece of communication in the game.
- Drag-and-drop placement felt natural and responsive on first touch, no fumbling.
- The Blueprints permanent upgrade shop had clean, readable descriptions ("+8% damage per rank," "unlocks the Braced Crate — twice the HP of plain wood") and visible cost scaling that made "what should I buy next" an easy, satisfying decision.
- Daily Missions claim animation (coin shower) was a genuine dopamine moment.
- Star coins persisting across runs (538 carried into Run 2) made restarting feel like real progress rather than a full reset — this is the single strongest retention hook in the game.
- CALL WAVE / CALL BOSS buttons gave a sense of player agency over pacing once discovered.

## Quit points (moments a real player like you would likely have closed the tab)
- **~0-4s:** If the "MONSTERS INCOMING" banner and glowing card hadn't been so visually obvious, the total absence of any instructions would have been a hard bounce for a genuinely impatient hyper-casual player. I only continued because the single glowing prompt was unmissable.
- **~334s (Run 1 first death):** The sudden unexplained "Destroyed!" with zero feedback on what killed me is exactly the kind of moment that makes a hyper-casual player feel the loss was arbitrary/unfair rather than earned — a strong quit risk if the free continue button hadn't been sitting right there (and I only found it by accidental curiosity).
- **~523s (Run 2 death, worse than Run 1):** Regressing from wave 13 to wave 8 with no clear explanation of why (I was playing "better" by spending stars up front) would frustrate a player trying to interpret the game's skill/reward logic — a second unexplained loss back-to-back is a realistic tab-close moment.

## Would you play a 3rd run? (1–5, why, and the single change that would raise it)
**3/5.** The core placement-and-merge loop is fun to watch and the persistent star economy genuinely pulled me into a second run. But dying twice with zero explanation of the cause, combined with several unexplained UI elements (inert FREE card, ambiguous 3x button, no personal HP bar), left me without the confidence that improving my play would reliably improve my results — that uncertainty is what would make me put the game down rather than opening a third run.
**Single change that would raise it:** Show a visible health/integrity number for the player's own base/gate at all times (not just a vague red vignette), and flash a brief "the Gate was destroyed by [enemy]" or equivalent cause-of-death message on the Destroyed screen.
