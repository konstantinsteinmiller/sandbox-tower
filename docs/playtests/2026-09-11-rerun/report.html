Tower Siege Playtest Rerun

The three testers who had the hardest time in the first playtest played the new build blind: the same personas, the same prompts, two runs each. This page shows what the fixes changed and what still stops a first-timer.

3 testers · 6 runs · 27 min of game time D1 casual browser (desktop) · P6 hyper-casual (phone) · P10 explorer (phone) Compared against the same personas in the first run

The phone's first minute is fixed. In the first run no phone tester finished the tutorial, and P10 needed four minutes to work out the controls. This time both phone testers placed a piece within 4–7 seconds, finished the tutorial and saw the merge lesson within 16 seconds. Neither reported a frozen game or "tapping attacks enemies".

The upgrade tree now gets found and used. Both phone testers found it within half a minute and bought 12–15 ranks, up from 8 and 1. All three claimed their daily missions; before, no phone tester had.

Losses are still unexplained. All three still ended a run without knowing why the tower fell. The desktop's out-of-reach cavalry killed D1 twice. The free gift card, from the other session's chest pass, didn't respond for two testers.

What the pass fixed

Verified in play

PhoneFixed

The first lesson now shows the move

  • The selected card is live and lit, with the demo's touch dot on it; the lit column still has its arrow. P6: "the single glowing prompt was unmissable". Controls were understood in 4–7 s, against 48 s and 238 s before.
  • Both phone saves record the tutorial as finished and the merge lesson as seen. No tester reported the old "frozen" dim or the "tap to attack" misreading.
  • The "protect the gate" picture under the banner (monsters → gate with a heart) gave P10 the goal at ~2 s. P6 still says the goal was never stated.
  • The merge lesson fired on each tester's first real merge, and P6 called it "the single clearest piece of information in the whole session".

Upgrades found and bought

Bottom-bar button found in 22–30 s on both phones. P6 bought the +50% HP first rank at once; the phone testers ended with 12 and 15 ranks, against 8 and 1 before.

Missions claimed by everyone

The claimable badge and the phone corner button led all three to a 200-coin claim, where before no phone tester claimed any.

The defeat screen pulls

D1 called it "the single best-designed motivational moment", and its rank climbed #234 → #164 between defeats. The revive's 1× token was noticed.

Danger cues fire

The gate-hit edge wash and red gate outline were P6's "only danger signal". The crosshair and the one-gun tier art are visible in D1's frames.

What still stops a first-timer

Five gaps left, worst first

CriticalBoth 3 / 3

Nobody knows why the tower fell

Every defeat was "sudden". P10 watched the wall go from full to rubble in about 15 s under fire, frost and flyers with no breakdown. P6 wanted a number for the base's health. D1 couldn't connect lost blocks to the gate bar, which refills between waves.

Fix

Show the killer on the defeat screen as a picture: the enemy portrait that landed the final blow, beside the fallen gate. Give the gate bar visible segments or a number, and explain status effects the first time they land, with a fire or frost icon over the struck block.

HighDesktop 1 / 1 desktop

The out-of-reach cavalry arrives unannounced at wave 14

It killed D1 twice, once even after D1 bought +80% range. In the frame before death the cues all fired, but the crosshair sits on the screen edge by an enemy barely in view, and the tray card's hover tooltip covers the red "out of reach" hint row.

Fix

Preview one standoff enemy around wave 4–6, so the answer (cavalry or range) is learned before it's needed. Keep the crosshair on screen with an edge arrow when the enemy is off-frame. Hide tray tooltips while the reach hint is up.

HighBoth 2 / 3

The free gift card doesn't respond

From the chest pass: the gold "FREE" tile with its count badge "never did anything when clicked or dragged" for D1 and P6. D1 ended the session with three gifts (two frost, one mortar) still waiting. A rejected gift placement is silent, just like the old hand-piece rejection.

Fix

The other session is already moving the gift into the tray's first slot and dropping the hand's selected piece when the gift is tapped. After that lands, give the gift the same green legal-cell flash hand pieces now get. Check it in play.

MediumPhone 2 / 2 phone

Missions surface only after wave 8–10

The corner button waits until a mission is claimable, and none finishes early (60 kills, 15 blocks). P6: "a player who dies before wave 8 never sees this hook."

Fix

Make one of the day's missions finishable in waves 1–2 ("hold 2 waves"), or show the corner button with a progress ring from the start of the first run.

MediumBoth 3 / 3

Buttons that spend coins without showing what they buy

Reinforced Hand took 30 of P10's 36 coins with no visible effect. "Upgrades are permanent" still first appears after a death, because the tree intro only runs when the tree is opened from the defeat screen. The revive read as a fast-forward to D1 and as "no stated value" to P10.

Fix

Run the intro's first step on the first open of the tree, from anywhere. On Reinforced Hand, flip the three tray cards gold in place as the preview, and confirm before it spends coins. Put a mini tower-rebuilt picture on the revive button.

How this was run

Method and caveats

  • Testers. Three Claude Sonnet agents with the first run's personas and prompts, blind, screenshots only, mouse (D1) or touch (P6, P10). Game time was held between actions, as before.
  • Harness changes. Pacing guidance was built in from the start (10-minute cap per run). The desktop screenshots now draw the mouse pointer, so the placement ghost no longer reads as a stray block.
  • Build. A snapshot of the main tree at 14:45: the six-agent pass plus the other session's edits up to then. It predates that session's 15:18–15:40 work on the gift tile and a tutorial hand.
  • Four browser closures, not game crashes. Between 15:28 and 15:33, all three Chrome windows and one restart were closed by something outside the test: another process or a window close. Each was reopened on the same save (P10 lost one block), and a supervisor now reopens killed windows automatically.
  • Small sample. This is three people's worth of signal, chosen because they struggled most last time. Read the direction, not the averages.
  • No audio, as before.
Raw material is in the repo at docs/playtests/2026-09-11-rerun/: three tester reports, their notes, and the harness with its supervisor. The first run's material is next to it in 2026-09-11-blind/.
