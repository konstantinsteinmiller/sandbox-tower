# Playtester 5 — desktop, persona: non-gamer adult

## Summary (5 lines max)
This is a tower-defense/merge game where you build a wall of blocks (weapons, resource huts, filler crates) out of a hand of cards, then survive waves of monsters. The core loop is easy to fall into by copying tutorial arrows, and the permanent "Tech Tree" between runs is a genuine, well-explained incentive once you find it. But big chunks of information arrive too late (currency explanations, the "banners" mechanic) or never arrive at all (what actually destroyed my wall, what the red wave-bar means, why run 2 went worse than run 1 despite upgrades). Card placement was fiddly enough that I frequently couldn't tell if my click had worked. I survived to wave 10 in run 1 and only wave 7 in run 2, and never understood why the second run was worse.

## Run 1 — what happened
- **~2s**: Game opened straight into the tower-building screen, no menu, no title explanation. Text "MONSTERS INCOMING!" with "Wave 0" told me something would attack soon. Top bar showed three unlabeled resource counts (wood, stone, a third icon at 0). An arrow pointed at a highlighted hand card "REINFORCED HAND 30" — I followed it because it was the only thing telling me what to do.
- **~3-5s**: Clicking the card put me into a placement mode with a grid over the wall; I clicked the highlighted cell and a full-screen popup then explained merging (two tier-1 icons combine into a tier-2 green icon, etc.) purely in pictures, no words like "merge" or "upgrade" ever appeared.
- **~8-10s**: A second tutorial popup compared a plain cannon (-10%) to a merged cannon (-75%) hitting an enemy's health bar, teaching that merging = more damage. Clicking the placed cannon opened a detail panel with real numbers (HP, DAMAGE, COOLDOWN, RANGE, SPLASH) and an UPGRADE button — the clearest, best piece of UI in the game.
- **~12-30s (Wave 1)**: First wave (3 enemies) resolved almost entirely on its own; I barely had to act. Resources grew automatically between waves.
- **~1 min (waves 2-4)**: I explored all 5 hand cards by clicking them — each showed a tooltip with name, size, HP/damage and a one-line description (e.g. "Wood Crate: cheap filler, sometimes gives a bonus Archery unit that hits fliers"). Built out both sides of the wall with crates, an archery tower, and a Lumber Hut.
- **~52s**: A red "SPEND!" badge pointed me to a green diamond button, which opened a "TECH TREE" — a permanent upgrade menu. Bought "Foundations" (+6% block HP) for 20 gold. This was the first time I understood the 3rd top-bar resource was a spendable currency.
- **~3 min (waves 5-7)**: Discovered "Daily Missions" (clipboard icon, red badge) — three already-completed goals gave a big coin-shower reward (180 gold). Spent it on more tech-tree nodes (Sharpened Bolts +14% damage, Lumber Stockpile).
- **~4:40 (wave 8)**: The entire right side of my tower (an archery unit and its support crate) was destroyed. I didn't see the moment it happened — only noticed after the fact when comparing screenshots. No warning drew my eye to it.
- **~5:20 (wave 9→10)**: The "CALL WAVE" button turned into a red "CALL BOSS" button — a genuinely clear warning. I rebuilt the destroyed side with stone blocks.
- **~5:30-5:58 (Wave 10, the boss)**: A large tree/plant boss appeared on the right with its own health bar. Fighting was chaotic — multiple enemies, a climbing fox-like enemy jumping onto the wall itself, my newly-built stone blocks taking heavy damage. The screen suddenly cut to a zoomed-in "sell panel" view of my tower, then to the results screen.
- **RUN 1 END (~5:58, ~358s)**: "DESTROYED! YOU SURVIVED TO WAVE 10 — NEW RECORD! +185 gold. Best Score 118. Rank #301 of 361." I never got a clear "you lost because X" moment — no message said the gate fell, or my HP hit zero, or I ran out of blocks.

## Run 2 — what happened
- Clicked the blue "skip forward" button on the results screen; it instantly started a fresh run (Wave 0, tower reset to just the gate, wood/stone/gold reset to starting values) while the permanent star-coin currency (152) carried over, exactly as a tutorial popup on the Tech Tree had explained moments earlier.
- I built faster this time (wood crates, an archery unit, a stone block, a lumber hut) since I already knew the card menu, and spent gold on Tech Tree nodes between waves (Foundations rank 3, Stone Stockpile) whenever the badge appeared.
- Waves 1-6 went smoothly, resources climbed into the 100s/200s, I claimed another Daily-Mission-style reward mid-run.
- **~10:10 (wave 6)**: Noticed the small wave-progress bar (top-left) turn solid red mid-fight — the same visual I'd seen right before losing run 1. This time nothing bad happened and the wave was held normally, so whatever that red bar means, it isn't a reliable "you're about to lose" signal — but I still don't know what it does mean.
- **~11:12 (wave 7)**: Fighting boars and orc-type enemies simultaneously from both sides; the wave counter suddenly cut to the results screen.
- **RUN 2 END (~11:13, wave 7)**: "DESTROYED! YOU SURVIVED TO WAVE 7. +67 gold. Best Score 118 (70 this run). Rank #301 of 361." A new tip appeared: "Banners boost every block they touch — and they multiply. Surround one gun instead of spreading them out." I never saw, was offered, or placed a "banner" item in either run, so this tip described a mechanic I have no way to use. I finished 3 waves worse than run 1 despite having more permanent upgrades, and the game gave no explanation why.

## Information gaps
1. **What actually kills you / ends a run.** Both runs cut from mid-fight chaos straight to the "DESTROYED" results screen with no message like "the gate fell" or "wall breached." I never learned the actual lose condition. Seen: throughout, most acutely at run 1 end (~358s) and run 2 end (~673s). Screenshot: `0096-click.jpg`, `0147-wait.jpg`. Severity: **high**.
2. **Two different gold-colored currencies look alike.** The in-run third resource (top-center, starts at 0, used for Tech Tree during a siege from wave rewards) and the permanent "star coin" (top-right, carries between runs, also spent in the Tech Tree) are visually similar (both gold coin icons) and I conflated them for a long time. Seen from ~2s onward; only clarified for me around ~360s when the Tech Tree tutorial explicitly said "coins come from every siege, win or lose." Screenshot: `0058-click.jpg`. Severity: **high**.
3. **Card placement did not reliably confirm on click.** Multiple times I clicked a highlighted (red-outlined) placement cell and nothing happened — resources stayed unchanged — until I clicked again or clicked a slightly different pixel. I could never tell whether "red outline" meant "ready to place here" or "invalid, occupied." Seen repeatedly, worst around ~292-298s (screenshots `0079` through `0086`). Severity: **high**.
4. **Tech Tree currency and permanence rules explained only after run 1 ended.** "Coins come from every siege, win or lose," "everything you buy is permanent," and the gold/blue/dim node color coding were all explained via a 3-step tutorial the very first time I opened the shopping-cart icon — which happened to be after my first full run. Would have made the whole first run far less confusing if shown earlier. Screenshot: `0097-click.jpg`, `0098-click.jpg`, `0099-click.jpg`. Severity: **medium**.
5. **"Banners" mechanic mentioned only in a post-run tip, never encountered in play.** The run-2 result screen's tip said banners "boost every block they touch and multiply," but I never saw a banner card, banner icon, or any explanation of how to get one in either run. Screenshot: `0147-wait.jpg`. Severity: **medium**.
6. **Red wave-progress bar meaning is unclear/inconsistent.** It turned solid red in run 2 wave 6 without anything bad happening, so it isn't a simple "about to lose" indicator, but no tooltip or label ever explained what it tracks. Screenshot: `0140-wait.jpg`. Severity: **medium**.
7. **Destroyed wall sections give no warning.** In run 1 (wave 8) and again in run 2, whole sections of the tower (archery unit + supporting crates) were wiped out between one check-in and the next with no on-screen alert, flashing icon, or camera nudge toward the danger. I only noticed by comparing screenshots after the fact. Screenshot: `0073-wait.jpg`. Severity: **high**.
8. **Units/mystery icon behavior.** A small orange fox/bird creature jumped directly onto the wall platform itself (not staying on the ground path like other enemies) with no label explaining whether it's a "flier" or "climber" or how it differs from ground units. Screenshot: `0086-click.jpg`. Severity: **low**.
9. **Post-defeat offer buttons are unlabeled.** The results screen shows a green "WAVE 9 +100/+100 for 30 [currency]" button and (in run 2) an orange "3x [star-coin] for 30 [currency]" button, both without any explanatory text on hover or otherwise — I could not tell what either actually does before spending. Screenshot: `0105-click.jpg` (green), `0149-hover.jpg` (orange). Severity: **medium**.
10. **A stray, unresponsive red-outlined tile "ghost."** Around 120s a leftover single-tile placement highlight appeared floating alone in the water area below the tower, unrelated to anything I was doing, and did not respond to clicks; it persisted for several minutes across screens. Looked like a UI bug rather than intentional. Screenshot: `0044-click.jpg`. Severity: **low**.

## Incentive gaps
1. **No clear "why did run 2 go worse" feedback.** I entered run 2 with strictly more permanent upgrades than I started run 1 with, yet survived 3 fewer waves, and the game offered zero explanation (e.g., "harder wave mix," "you built less efficiently"). This undercuts the core promise of the meta-progression system — that spending gold should make you stronger — because I couldn't verify it was working. Peaked right when I opened the Tech Tree in run 1 (felt genuinely rewarding), dropped hard at run 2's result screen. Severity for motivation: **high**.
2. **Daily Missions were easy to miss entirely.** All three missions were already complete by the time I found them (via a small red badge on an unlabeled clipboard icon) and gave a large lump of gold. A first-time player who doesn't notice small badges could go an entire session without claiming free rewards that clearly matter a lot to progression. Screenshot: `0056-click.jpg`. Severity: **medium**.
3. **No visible long-term goal beyond "beat your record."** Both runs' result screens show "Best Score" and a leaderboard rank, which is a nice comparative hook, but there's no visible campaign, unlock list, or "next boss preview" telling me what I'm working toward over many runs — just an abstract wave counter climbing.
4. **Losing a wall section felt arbitrary rather than earned.** Because destroyed sections gave no warning (see Info Gap 7), losing defenses felt like something that happened to me rather than a result of a choice I made (e.g., "you under-armored the right flank"), which reduces the feeling that better play would fix it.
5. **The "reinforced hand" refresh timer/cost (30) was never explained.** A card labeled "REINFORCED HAND 30" sat above the hand of cards the entire time and I never learned what clicking it costs or gives — I avoided it because I didn't understand it, possibly missing content.

## What worked
- The very first placement + merge-comparison tutorial (cannon -10% vs -75% damage shown on an actual enemy) was the single clearest teaching moment in the whole game — visual, concrete, and immediately useful.
- Clicking any placed tower piece or hand card opened a detailed tooltip/panel with real numbers and a one-line plain-English description — once discovered, this made almost every item understandable.
- The Tech Tree's own onboarding (once opened) was excellent: three short, plain-language popups covering currency source, permanence, and node color coding.
- The "CALL WAVE" button turning into a red "CALL BOSS" button before wave 10 was a clean, unambiguous warning that something different was coming.
- Daily Missions' claim animation (a shower of coin icons) felt satisfying and was the biggest single reward moment in either run.
- Restarting after defeat was frictionless — one click on the results screen dropped me straight back into a fresh, fully-reset run with permanent currency intact.

## Quit points
- **~4:40 into run 1 (wave 8), after losing a wall section I didn't see fall.** If I hadn't been specifically tasked with playing to defeat, this is the moment a real first-time player in my position — already unsure what half the icons meant — might feel the game is "just taking things away for no reason" and close the tab.
- **End of run 2 (~11:13, wave 7, worse than run 1).** Getting a worse score on my second attempt despite spending currency on upgrades is the strongest single demotivator I hit. A player like me, who doesn't naturally trust "it'll pay off eventually," would likely stop here rather than risk a third disappointing run.

## Would you play a 3rd run? (1-5, why, and the single change that would raise it)
**3 / 5.** The permanent Tech Tree and the restart-immediately flow are genuinely good hooks, and I don't dislike the game — but the confusion about currencies, silent losses of tower sections, and (especially) run 2 doing worse than run 1 despite upgrades left me unsure whether continuing would actually make me better at this. The single change that would raise this most: **tell me, in plain words, exactly why I lost each run** (e.g., "the north wall fell after 4 hits" or "the gate reached 0 HP"), so losing feels like a lesson I can act on rather than something that just happened to me.
