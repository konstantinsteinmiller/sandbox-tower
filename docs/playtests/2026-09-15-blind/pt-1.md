# Blind Playtest #1 — Tower Siege (Desktop)

Persona: 7-year-old, wants explosions and monsters NOW, doesn't read long text, clicks the biggest/brightest/most animated thing.

Game-clock budget used: ~165s of 180s.

## 1. Persona & first 30 seconds

At 1.5s the screen said "MONSTERS INCOMING!" with a big yellow arrow pointing at a glowing sword icon at the bottom. I thought this was going to be a fighting game where I click a sword to attack. I clicked the glowing thing (shot 0001/0002) and it turned out to be a tower-building slot — a grid appeared over the castle. I clicked the highlighted grid cell (shot 0003) and instead of anything blowing up, I got dropped into a totally different screen: two cannons side by side labeled "-10%" and "-75%" with red/yellow bars draining (shot 0003/0004). I had no idea what I was looking at or what I was supposed to do — I just clicked the blue-highlighted one because it was brighter. Only after that did the game say "CLICK TO CONTINUE" and drop me back into the castle view with a green cannon placed. So my first 30 seconds were: expected a fight, got a placement puzzle, got a confusing stat-comparison screen I didn't understand, and only THEN saw my first tower.

## 2. Quit moment

I never fully wanted to close the tab, but I came closest twice:
- ~47-68s (shots 0016, 0021, 0036/0037): the "TECH TREE" screen. It's a big wall of tiny grey icons, almost all locked/greyed out, with a long sentence ("Everything you buy here is permanent. It carries into every future run.") that I skipped past without reading. I bought two things ("Foundations", "Sharpened Bolts") basically blind — I don't know what they really do, I just clicked the glowing one and then the gold button under it.
- ~164s (shot 0054): right after the wave-10 boss fight, the camera suddenly zoomed way into the wall with a "SELL +3" popup covering everything, and two goblins plus a skeleton guy just standing still at the bottom doing nothing. That jump-cut was disorienting — I didn't do anything to cause it and didn't know what state the game was in.

If I'd kept playing solo (no adult telling me to push through), the tech tree screen is where I'd have wandered off to do something else, because nothing was moving and I didn't understand the icons.

## 3. Boredom log

- **~8-15s** (shots 0003-0005): stat-comparison screen with "-10%"/"-75%" — nothing to look at but two icons and draining bars, no monster, no motion, just me clicking blind.
- **~47-68s** (shots 0016, 0021, 0036-0038): Tech Tree popups — twice I opened this via the glowing green button and got the exact same wall of icons both times. Nothing moves, nothing explains itself without clicking each tiny icon one at a time.
- Between waves whenever I was just sitting on the castle-editing screen waiting for the "CALL WAVE" timer (e.g. shots 0006-0008, 0014, 0020, 0024, 0027, 0039) — the screen is static except a countdown number ticking, no motion, no monsters. I had to actively hunt for something to click (repair button, upgrade panel) to not be bored.

## 4. Bugs & inconsistencies

- **Unlabeled stat-comparison screen** — shot `0003-wait.jpg` / `0004-wait.jpg` (game-second ~6-9): two cannon icons with "-10%" and "-75%" and draining red/yellow bars, with zero explanatory text about what stat is changing or why I'm choosing between them. Looks like a merge/reroll mechanic but nothing on screen says so.
- **Tech tree opened as an "ability" button but is really a meta/permanent-upgrade shop** — the button that flagged with a red number badge (shots 0016, 0021, 0036) always opened the identical Tech Tree screen, never anything "abilit"-like (like a special power). The badge count going up (1 → 2 → 3 → 4...) made me think something new was ready, but it was the same screen every time.
- **Disorienting hard camera cut** — shot `0054-wait.jpg` (game-second ~164): after wave 10 ended, the view suddenly zoomed in tight on the wall/gate stack with a "SELL +3" popup covering the middle, no transition. I couldn't tell if this was a menu, a bug, or part of the fight.
- **Tower appears to visually disappear mid-fight** — shot `0053-wait.jpg` (game-second ~161): during wave 10, the left cannon tower slot shows a blank/broken white square where a cannon used to be, with a red highlight box next to it — looked like the tower got destroyed, but there was no clear "tower destroyed!" message, explosion animation, or confirmation, it just silently became a placeholder icon.
- **Top-left HP bar dropped to near-empty/red by wave 9-10** (shots 0050-0053) with no popup or warning telling me my main HP/castle health was in danger — I only noticed because the color changed, and I'm not sure if the game ever considers you to have "lost" a segment permanently.

## 5. Unreadable / unclear

- The `-10%` / `-75%` comparison screen (shot 0004) — no idea what stat, no units, no name for what's happening.
- Tech tree icons (shots 0016, 0036) — dozens of tiny monochrome icons in a tree, no names visible until clicked one at a time. As a kid I'd never explore this screen on my own.
- Cannon info panel (shot 0007): "HP 120/120", "DAMAGE 48", "COOLDOWN 1.45", "RANGE 8.5", "SPLASH 1.2" — none of these numbers have units (seconds? tiles? %?). I don't know if 8.5 range is good or bad.
- The three resource counters at the top (wood log icon, stone brick icon, gear/scrap icon) are never explained — I just watched the numbers go up and never learned what buys what.
- "HOARD +25%" tag that appeared near the Call Wave button (shot 0046) — never explained, I don't know what "hoard" means as a 7-year-old.

## 6. What kept me playing

The explosions. Every time a cannon or the lightning tower hit a monster, big glowing burst animations and floating damage numbers (like "+51", "437") popped up, and switching to 3x speed made multiple explosions happen back-to-back, which was genuinely fun to watch (shots 0032, 0041, 0044, 0046, 0048). The "CALL BOSS" button and the sky turning dark red for wave 10 (shot 0052) was the most exciting moment in the whole session — that's the kind of big dramatic change that grabs attention. The daily-missions "CLAIM" button giving me coins instantly (shot 0026) was also a nice small reward hit.

## 7. Top 3 changes I'd make

1. **Cut or radically simplify the post-placement stat-comparison screen** (the "-10%/-75%" cannon picker). It fires immediately after a brand-new player places their very first tower, with zero context, and it's the single most confusing moment in the whole session.
2. **Hide or heavily simplify the Tech Tree for early play.** Right now it's a dense grid of tiny unlabeled icons that a first-time (or young) player will either ignore or click blindly. Either gate it behind a later wave, or show 3-4 big, clearly-labeled cards instead of a whole tree.
3. **Never hard-cut the camera without a clear cause.** The zoom-in-with-SELL-popup after wave 10 (shot 0054) came out of nowhere and made me feel like something had broken. Any big view change should have a visible trigger (a button I pressed, a clear "wave complete, rebuild your wall!" banner) rather than snapping silently.
