# Playtester 10 — mobile, persona: curious explorer

## Summary (5 lines max)
A tower-defense/merge game: you drop crates into 3 lanes below a wall, they auto-merge into cannons/archer towers that shoot approaching monsters, and a "gate" in the middle must survive each wave. I never got an explicit tutorial telling me any of this — I inferred the mechanics through trial and error (tapping the tower revealed a merge chart; tapping empty lane slots, not the item card, is what actually places a block). The core loop is legible once you find it, and the meta-progression (a persistent star currency + permanent "Blueprints" upgrades, daily missions, a boss every ~8 waves) gives real reasons to keep playing. My main friction was invisible interaction rules (which tap target does what) and towers that silently died and needed constant rebuilding with no alert.

## Run 1 — what happened (timeline with game-time stamps)
- **~0 s**: Game opened already mid-tutorial (Wave 1.2, "MONSTERS INCOMING!" banner, arrow pointing at a highlighted crate icon in the bottom bar). No prior title/menu screen, no explanation of the goal.
- **~1-5 s**: Tapped the highlighted crate slot — it placed a crate in the leftmost lane, which after a moment turned into a cannon tower. No message told me this was a "merge," I just saw the shape change.
- **~10 s**: Tapped my own tower out of curiosity — this opened a "merge chart" popup showing white→green→blue→purple cannon tiers. This was the single clearest piece of teaching in the whole run, but it's opt-in and easy to miss (a first-timer who doesn't poke the tower never sees it).
- **~11-15 s**: Game cut to a close-up combat view showing two goblins with floating "-10%" / "-75%" damage numbers over health bars — first real feedback on what my tower does to enemies.
- **~17-25 s**: Discovered "VOLLEY" (an ability button with a charge counter) and a green diamond icon that opens a "BLUEPRINTS" permanent-upgrade shop (paid in a star currency). Also found "Daily Missions" via a clipboard icon (three goals: survive to wave 2, defeat 60 enemies, place 15 blocks — cash rewards for each).
- **~33-45 s**: Learned by accident that resources climb passively over time (not just from combat), and that item costs/types on the bottom bar cycle after each placement.
- **~150-155 s**: Wave 8 introduced a visually distinct "boss" — a glowing tree creature with a crown icon and its own health bar, dramatically spotlighted with darkened surroundings. It destroyed my gate. **Run 1 ended: "Destroyed! You survived to wave 8 — NEW RECORD — Rank #321 of 361", +77 star currency.**
- The defeat screen offered a one-time free "continue from Wave 7" option (cost shown as gear+star icons but it did not visibly deduct anything) — I took it, rebuilt, and died to the same tree boss again a few waves later, ending at the same wave 8 / rank #321 with no further continue offered.

## Run 2 — what happened
Reloaded the game (fresh title state) after spending accumulated stars on two permanent Blueprints upgrades (Foundations +50% tower HP, Sharpened Bolts +8% damage) — these persisted across the restart, confirmed as real meta-progression. This time I placed blocks by tapping directly on the empty lane silhouette rather than the item card (a mechanic I'd only worked out mid-Run-1), which sped things up. Reached Wave 5 (5 enemies remaining) with the gate critically damaged (pink/red outline) and all three towers destroyed and not yet rebuilt when the session's 300-second time cap was reached. **TIME UP at Wave 5, 5 enemies left; gate not yet fallen.** I felt some urgency/frustration in this moment — I wanted one more block-placement action to shore up the gate but ran out of time first.

## Information gaps (numbered)
1. **What "MONSTERS INCOMING!" / the goal of the game actually is.** Never explained in words at any point I saw — inferred purely from watching enemies walk toward a wall and a health-looking bar. Never fully clarified (what happens if the gate falls exactly? Game over, confirmed only after actually losing). Stayed unclear for the entire session. Screenshot: `0001-shot.jpg`. Severity: high.
2. **How to place a block.** Tapping the bottom-bar item card only *selects/highlights* it; the actual placement requires tapping the empty lane slot separately. I spent several early actions tapping the card repeatedly with no effect before discovering this by accident (`0043-tap.jpg`, ~83 s). Stayed unclear for ~80 seconds of real confusion. Severity: high.
3. **The merge mechanic itself.** Only discoverable by tapping an already-built tower, which is not signposted — nothing tells you towers are tappable for info. Screenshot: `0008-tap.jpg` (~10 s). Severity: medium.
4. **What the two non-star currencies (wood-crate icon, gear icon) are for/generated by.** They climb passively and from combat; I never saw an explicit source or a clear "this currency buys that item" label distinguishing them from the block costs. Stayed unclear the whole session. Severity: medium.
5. **The "FREE" gem item slot with a red badge counter (e.g. "3").** Tapped it multiple times at what looked like the right location with no visible effect or currency change. Never resolved. Screenshot: `0031-tap.jpg` (~48 s). Severity: low.
6. **Why towers randomly disappear.** Towers I built would vanish between screenshots with no on-screen death animation caught, no clear "your tower was destroyed" callout, no health bar visible on the towers themselves in the top-down view (only in the rare close-up view). I had to infer loss purely from the crate silhouette reverting to a dashed empty outline. This made rebuilding feel like whack-a-mole rather than a readable resource decision. Screenshots: `0038-wait.jpg`→`0040-wait.jpg`. Severity: high.
7. **What the winged flying chest icon does.** Appeared repeatedly drifting across the top of the screen; tapping it did nothing detectable, and it just flew off screen. Never explained. Screenshot: `0027-tap.jpg`. Severity: low.
8. **The "REINFORCED HAND, 30" button.** Persistent prompt with a coin cost that toggled to a checkmark when tapped but produced no observable resource change or clear effect (item cards got a highlighted border). Never understood what it actually did. Severity: medium.
9. **The right-side no-entry/crossed-circle icons that appeared over empty lanes in Run 2 late-game** (`0087-wait.jpg`) — never explained; guessed it meant "no tower here to fire" but not confirmed by any tooltip.

## Incentive gaps (numbered)
1. **No visible reason given up front for why wave count / rank matters.** The "Rank #321 of 361" comparison on defeat is a nice competitive hook, but it's the first time any leaderboard/comparison is mentioned — nothing earlier in the session primed me to care about climbing it. Screenshot: `0059-wait.jpg`. Severity: medium.
2. **Repair/rebuild loop has no forward momentum feedback.** Since towers die silently and often, a chunk of each wave-gap was spent just replacing what I had before rather than building something new — this reads as treading water rather than progressing, which is a motivation drag around the wave 5-7 mark of both runs. Severity: medium.
3. **Daily missions are excellent but only surfaced through an unlabeled clipboard icon with a small red badge** — a first-time player who doesn't tap random UI elements (unlike me) could easily miss free currency sitting right there. Severity: low-medium.

## What worked (things that were clear or motivating)
- The permanent "Blueprints" shop (star currency, explicit "everything you buy here is permanent, it carries into every future run" message) was the single strongest motivator — clear, legible meta-progression that immediately made me want to keep playing for more stars.
- Daily missions with visible progress bars and clear coin rewards were a strong, well-labeled incentive once found.
- The Wave-8 boss reveal (crown icon, darkened screen, spotlight) was a genuinely exciting beat — best-executed moment of the whole session.
- The free "continue from Wave 7" offer on the first defeat screen was a great immediate retention save — I likely would have stopped at Run 1's first death otherwise, and it kept me playing without friction (no forced ad wait, no confusing menu).
- Currency numbers ticking up passively, "+X" floating text on kills/pickups, and the "NEW RECORD" banner all gave a satisfying steady drip of positive feedback.

## Quit points (moments a real player like you would likely have closed the tab)
- **~80-85 s into Run 1**: repeatedly tapping the shop card with zero effect and no error/shake feedback was genuinely frustrating — a less exploratory player than me plausibly quits here thinking the game is broken/unresponsive.
- **First real defeat (~150 s), before discovering the continue offer**: if the "WAVE 7 continue" option hadn't been there, this is a natural close-the-tab point — the "Destroyed!" screen alone, with only a reload icon, doesn't strongly pull you back in.
- **Mid Run 2, waves 5-6, when 2-3 towers were simultaneously missing and I couldn't tell why**: the "am I even doing anything right" feeling briefly outweighed the fun.

## Would you play a 3rd run? (1–5, why, and the single change that would raise it)
**3/5.** The merge-tower core loop and the Blueprints meta-progression are genuinely likeable and I was curious to push further — but the friction of silently dying towers and the "which tap does what" confusion cost real enjoyment and time. The single change that would raise this most: **give towers a visible health bar and a clear destroyed-notification/repair-prompt in the main top-down view**, so losing a tower is legible instead of something you notice only in hindsight by comparing screenshots.
