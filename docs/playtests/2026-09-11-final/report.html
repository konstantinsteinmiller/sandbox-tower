Tower Siege Final Playtest

The same three testers played the build with today's follow-ups blind again: the kill cam, the red gate bar, gift sockets, the starter mission, the first-open upgrade intro, the Reinforced Hand preview, the revive picture and the 3× cost chip. This time each had five minutes of game time.

3 testers · 15 min of game time · 5-minute cap each D1 casual browser (desktop) · P6 hyper-casual (phone) · P10 explorer (phone) Compared against the same personas in the first run and the rerun

The early hooks now land in the first minute. All three claimed the new "survive to wave 2" mission, P6 at 21 s and D1 at 61 s; in the rerun the first claims came at 150–230 s. All three read "everything you buy here is permanent" on their first visit to the upgrade screen, mid-run and before any defeat. D1 placed the free gift cannon, which the rerun's D1 never managed.

The kill cam explains a death when it's seen, but two of three missed it. P10 caught the crowned treant, wrote "it destroyed my gate" and called it the best moment of the session. D1 and P6 were in 5–10-second waits while it played and still describe deaths with "no warning". Towers that fall mid-wave are still invisible to both phone testers.

Two of the new buttons don't explain themselves yet. D1 read the revive as "a wave 9 completion reward" and was surprised to be back in the run. P6 paid 30 coins for Reinforced Hand and saw "the hand looked identical", because the gold flip had already happened on the preview tap.

What the follow-ups fixed

Verified in play

A mission in the first minute

The badge led all three to a 40-coin claim of "survive to wave 2", P6 at 21 s and D1 at 61 s. P6: "good daily-return incentive". Every tester also finished the 60-kill goal, and none came back to claim it.

"Permanent", said on first open

D1, P6 and P10 each quoted "everything you buy here is permanent" on their first mid-run visit to the upgrade screen. P10 called it "the single strongest motivator". Before, that line waited for a defeat.

The gift gets placed

D1: "after wave 3 the toolbar offered a FREE second cannon which I placed next to the first one". In the rerun D1 ended with three gifts it could never place.

The gate bar reads as health

D1 tracked it all run ("health stayed at 100% for 9 straight waves") and read it empty at the end. The rerun's D1 couldn't connect it to the tower. D1 also bought 3× at 154 s, and this time raised no cost complaint.

The kill cam names the killer

  • P10 caught it: the treant under the spotlight, crown on its head, the Gate gone beside it. "It destroyed my gate", and "the best-executed moment of the whole session".
  • P10 read it as a boss reveal, which it was here. On a plain grunt the crown may read as "boss" too.
  • D1 and P6 never saw it. See the first gap below.
What still stops a first-timer

Four gaps left, worst first

CriticalBoth 3 / 3

Losses are still easy to miss

D1 and P6 were each in a 5–10-second wait when the three-second kill cam played. D1's first view after the death was the first-death lesson zooming onto a block with "SELL +3", and D1 still reports that the boss broke the gate "with no visible warning". P6 and P10 noticed destroyed towers only "by diffing screenshots". The lost-block ghost lasts 1.2 s.

Fix

Put the killer on the defeat screen, its sprite with the crown beside "You survived to wave N", so the explanation outlives the three seconds. Leave rubble on lost cells until the next build phase, and add a broken-block tally to the "wave held" toast. Hold the first-death lesson until the defeat screen has been seen.

HighPhone 2 / 2 phone

"Arm, then tap a column" doesn't stick

P10 tapped the lit card three times, then its column at 10 s. The drop worked, but P10 credited the merge chart that followed to "tapping the tower". By its own account P10 then spent about 70 s tapping tray cards before settling on "tap the lane" at 83 s, and its taps on the FREE gift went nowhere for the same reason. P6 dragged from the start, which works. P6's "tapping the slot did nothing" is a harness artefact: that tap was typed into a note and never sent.

Fix

When a card is armed and the next tap lands on the tray again, replay the demo's ghost from that card into a legal column, and pulse the landing cells while anything is armed. Or let a second tap on the armed card drop it into the lit column.

MediumBoth 3 / 3

Reinforced Hand still doesn't say what it buys

The preview works: nobody was charged by accident. But P6 tapped twice and paid, and since the cards had already flipped gold on the first tap, "the hand looked identical". P10 saw the tick and the gold borders and never learned the effect. D1 never tapped it, so it read as a label.

Fix

Show the numbers in the preview with a "+HP / +dmg" chip on every card. The phone tray has none, and the desktop shows damage on guns only. Give the payment its own moment too: a sparkle and a chip pop on each card.

MediumBoth 2 / 3

The revive reads as a reward

D1 read "WAVE 9 +100 +100 30" as "a wave 9 completion reward to claim", then thought the restart button had resumed the siege. P6 used it with "no explanation of cost or effect" until the tap. Only P10 read it as a continue. At button size the rebuilt Gate is a small square in a white ring, and the resource bonus is what reads.

Fix

Let the picture carry it: the fallen tower's rubble rebuilding into the tower, large, left of the price, with the resource bonus as a small "+" line underneath.

How this was run

Method and caveats

  • Testers. Three Claude Sonnet agents with the same personas and prompts as the rerun, blind, screenshots only, mouse (D1) or touch (P6, P10). Game time was held between actions, as before.
  • Five-minute cap. The controller enforced it: waits were clamped to the limit and input was refused after it. All three stopped at 299–300 s. P6 and P10 got a second run; D1's "run 2" was the revive.
  • Build. A snapshot of the main tree at 18:15, with today's follow-ups. It was a development build, so perks cost coins rather than videos, and the leaderboard was blocked (ranks come from the baked snapshot).
  • Short cues fall between screenshots. When idle, the testers checked back every 8–12 s, so cues shorter than that could be missed: the three-second kill cam, the half-second gate strobe, the 1.2-second lost-block ghost. A player watching the screen would see more, and one glancing away would see about the same. The first gap leans on this.
  • One harness artefact. P6's "tapping the lit slot did nothing" came from a tap typed into a note command, so it was never sent.
  • Clean run. No page errors, and no browser closures or relaunches this time.
