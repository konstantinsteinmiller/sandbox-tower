# Playtester 6 — mobile, persona: hyper-casual phone player

## Summary (5 lines max)
A merge/tower-defense hybrid: drag cards from a hand onto lane slots, matching pieces auto-merge into stronger towers, survive waves to earn gold/gems/star-coins. The core loop is genuinely satisfying once discovered (currency numbers climb fast, merges feel good, waves escalate into a boss), but the opening 15-20 seconds gave me no working input — the highlighted slot looked tappable but only drag worked, and I found that by trial and error, not by any prompt. Several economy elements (REINFORCED HAND, HOARD %, VOLLEY cooldown/cost, why a tower vanished) were never explained and I had to infer them from before/after screenshots. The death screen's one-time "continue from last wave" offer and the permanent Blueprints meta-shop are strong retention hooks, but both were also under-explained at the moment they mattered most.

## Run 1 — what happened (timeline with game-time stamps)
- **~0s (first impression):** Screen shows "WAVE 1-2", a "MONSTERS INCOMING!" banner, an arrow pointing down at one highlighted lane slot, and 3 cost-bearing cards at the bottom plus a "VOLLEY" button. No instructions on what to do. I tapped the highlighted slot — nothing happened (screenshot 0001-shot.jpg, 0002-shot.jpg).
- **~1-3s:** Tried dragging the selected card into the lane instead — this worked, placing a tower and consuming the card (0003-drag.jpg).
- **~3-6s:** Dragging a second card triggered an unprompted "merge chain" popup (cannon→green→blue→purple→orange) explaining the upgrade path, then a damage-comparison popup (base cannon -10% vs merged cannon -75% damage) (0004-drag.jpg, 0007-wait.jpg). Both were informative but felt like they interrupted rather than onboarded.
- **~8-15s:** Returned to the main grid, wave 1 played out automatically with towers firing at goblins; "WAVE 1 HELD!" banner appeared, a "CALL WAVE" timer with a shrinking bonus % appeared, and star-coins (a third currency) appeared for the first time (0011-drag.jpg through 0013-drag.jpg).
- **~13-22s:** A directional arrow pointed at a previously-unexplained icon (green diamond) — tapping it opened a "BLUEPRINTS" permanent meta-upgrade shop; I also found a "DAILY MISSIONS" panel via a badge on a clipboard icon (0017-tap.jpg, 0024-tap.jpg).
- **~1 min:** Waves 2-4 cleared smoothly, currencies climbing nicely (gold, gems, star-coins all rising), no visible damage taken.
- **~2 min (wave 7-8):** "CALL WAVE" button changed to red "CALL BOSS" — first boss telegraph. Boss wave 8 was tense: a tower's HP bar turned red, and after the wave I noticed (only by comparing screenshots) that a whole tower slot had been silently destroyed and reverted to empty. Big currency payout followed (star-coin jumped from 141 to 171-204 range).
- **~3 min (wave 10-11):** Defenses got overwhelmed — multiple tower slots showed "no-signal" destroyed icons and the gate glowed red. Used the VOLLEY button reactively (it turned out to have a 2-wave cooldown, shown only after use) which cleared the remaining enemies and saved wave 10. Wave 11 then ended it: "Destroyed!" — **"You survived to Wave 11 (New Record), Rank #299 of 361, +257 star-coins."**
- **Continue offer:** A button reading "WAVE 10 +240 [gold] 30 [gems] (1x)" sat on the death screen with no explanation of cost or effect. Tapping it silently continued the run from wave 10 with fresh basic towers and all currency intact — a genuine "revive," but I only learned what it did by tapping it.
- **True Run 1 end (~208-232s):** Continued run reached wave 11 again and died the same way — this time no continue button appeared (single use, confirmed only by its absence).

## Run 2 — what happened
Restarted via the blue reload icon on the death screen (star-coins/meta-currency carried over at 707+; gold/gems reset to 90/0). Before restarting I spent star-coins in the Blueprints shop on "Foundations" (+tower HP) — the purchase UI itself changed confusingly after buying (description switched from "+50% total" to "+8% per rank"). Run 2 started with an enemy already approaching before I'd placed a single tower, and the gate was damaged as early as wave 2 (much rockier start than Run 1, since Run 1's mid-run merges/upgrades didn't carry over — only the permanent meta-purchase did). Survived through wave 4 ("WAVE 4 HELD"), and was mid-wave 5 (5-11 enemies remaining, towers being destroyed again) when the session's 300s budget ran out. No clean death screen this time — **TIME UP at wave 5, 11 enemies remaining, gold 107 / gems 24 / star-coins 761.**

## Incentive gaps
1. **What kept me going:** Currency counters visibly climbing every few seconds (gold/gems/star-coins), plus "+X" floating reward text on every kill. This was consistently satisfying from ~10s onward and never really dropped during Run 1's climb to wave 11.
2. **Peak moment:** The boss wave 8 payout (star-coin nearly tripled in one wave) and the "New Record / Rank #299 of 361" framing on the death screen — a leaderboard-style comparison is motivating even in a solo session.
3. **Drop moment:** Discovering after the second "Destroyed!" that the continue offer doesn't reappear — the run just ends with two small icons (shop, restart) and no summary of "what you unlocked" or "what to try differently next time." A player who doesn't proactively open Blueprints would leave with no sense of why to come back.
4. **Gap:** Nothing on-screen ever framed a longer-term goal ("reach wave 20 for X", "next portal reward at Y star-coins"). All incentive was moment-to-moment (kill enemy → get coins) rather than session-to-session, aside from the Daily Missions panel, which I only found via an unexplained badge, not a prompt.
5. **Gap:** The permanent Blueprints tree (a genuinely strong retention hook — "everything you buy here is permanent, carries into every future run") is buried behind an icon with no first-run callout; a hyper-casual player who dies once and doesn't notice the badge might never discover the meta-progression that would justify a second run.

## What worked
- Drag-to-merge mechanic: once discovered, immediately satisfying and visually clear (matching pieces glow/merge with a "x2/x3" badge).
- Reward floaties (+3, +12, +40 etc.) on every kill — constant positive feedback.
- "CALL WAVE" countdown with a shrinking bonus % is a clear, well-communicated push to act before the timer, without being punishing.
- Boss telegraph (CALL WAVE → CALL BOSS turning red) was an effective, self-explanatory escalation cue.
- Death screen's rank-out-of-total ("#299 of 361") and "New Record" badge gave a sense of comparative progress even without other visible players.
- The one-time continue-from-last-wave offer is a strong "don't lose everything" safety net that kept me playing past what would otherwise have been a hard stop.

## Quit points
- **~0-5s:** If tapping the highlighted slot (the game's only on-screen instruction) hadn't been followed by trying a drag out of habit from similar games, I would have had no working input and closed the tab within the first 10 seconds. This is the single biggest risk point in the whole session.
- **~230s (after the second "Destroyed!"):** With no continue button left and no explicit "come back tomorrow for X" hook surfaced, a player less curious than me about the Blueprints icon would likely have stopped here — nothing on the death screen itself argues for a second run beyond "tap the shop icon" being a small blue-square guess.

## Would you play a 3rd run? (1-5, why, and the single change that would raise it)
**4/5.** The core loop earns real engagement once you're past the opening confusion — currency, merges, and the boss escalation are all doing their job, and I wanted to see wave 12+ when time ran out mid-run-2. The single change that would raise it to a 5: make the death screen actively sell the next run (e.g., "Spend your 761 star-coins in Blueprints to get further next time" with the shop pre-highlighted), instead of leaving that discovery to chance the way the tower-slot drag and the VOLLEY button also were.
